# Chris Petro — Senior Software Architect | Fractional CTO | Engineering Leader

Chris Petro is a senior technology leader and software architect with more than 25 years of experience building, modernizing, and scaling complex software products. He is equally comfortable partnering with executives to define product and technology roadmaps as he is working directly with engineering teams on architecture, data platforms, cloud infrastructure, and implementation.

Chris has worked successfully across both fast-moving small companies and large enterprise organizations, adapting his approach to the size, maturity, and constraints of the business. His experience spans real-time analytics, high-volume data platforms, cloud modernization, AI-enabled product development, IoT systems, highly available distributed architectures, and heavily regulated enterprise environments.

Led engineering and partnered with company leadership on the product and technology strategy behind a large-scale behavioral analytics platform. Architectural and infrastructure initiatives reduced cloud costs by more than half and generated more than seven figures in savings while substantially improving application performance, reliability, and scalability.

Designed highly available hot-hot, multi-region architectures, automated infrastructure and complex CI/CD processes, and helped eliminate millions of dollars in long-term infrastructure, maintenance, and operational costs.

Designed and developed analytics platforms that enabled traffic engineers to understand signal performance and improve traffic flow throughout cities. Built systems that collected data from distributed traffic controllers and IoT devices, ingested millions of data points into centralized platforms, and transformed that information into intuitive analytics, visualization, and decision-support tools.

**Adknowledge** - *Senior Software Engineer → Java Team Lead* (2010–2014)  
Worked within an R&D organization alongside statisticians and machine-learning specialists to develop systems for understanding and fingerprinting user behavior so advertising could be targeted more effectively. Also contributed to high-volume real-time bidding platforms that automatically matched advertisers and publishers through millions of low-latency auctions.
